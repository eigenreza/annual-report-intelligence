# Data sources

The tool reads the files listed below from this directory. The eight annual reports are third-party documents and are not included in the repository. Download each one from the link given here and save it under the exact name shown in the layout, so that it drops straight into place. The two news files are included.

Expected layout:

```
data/
  BMW/BMW_Annual_Report_2021.pdf
  BMW/BMW_Annual_Report_2022.pdf
  BMW/BMW_Annual_Report_2023.pdf
  Ford/Ford_Annual_Report_2021.pdf
  Ford/Ford_Annual_Report_2022.pdf
  Ford/Ford_Annual_Report_2023.pdf
  Tesla/Tesla_Annual_Report_2022.pdf
  Tesla/Tesla_Annual_Report_2023.pdf
  news.pdf      (included in the repository)
  news.docx     (included in the repository)
```

| BMW/BMW_Annual_Report_2023.pdf | BMW Finance N.V. Annual Report 2023 | https://www.bmwgroup.com/content/dam/grpw/websites/bmwgroup_com/ir/downloads/en/2024/Annual-Reports-other-Group-companies/BMW_Finance_NV_Annual_Report_2023.pdf |

The BMW files for 2022 and 2023 are the annual reports of BMW Finance N.V., a financing subsidiary, which is a different reporting entity from BMW Group. The 2021 file is the BMW Group report. The tool's document registry records this distinction and keeps the subsidiary's figures separate from group figures.
